01

WHY THIS SITE WINS

Owned land changes
everything.

Land economics

Lot is owner-controlled at 8480 Andermatt Dr — no lease drag, no host fee, no rent escalation. This was the single biggest negative in the Fallbrook model ($54K/yr).

Retail / gas / errand node

Sam's Club (with fuel), Walmart, Menards, Dairy Queen, restaurants — all already vehicle-oriented and dwell-friendly. Charging fits the existing trip pattern.

Confirmed DCFC gap

Owner has confirmed no DCFC around south/east Lincoln. The nearest open-network DCFC (Electrify America at Casey's NW 20th) is ~9 mi NW.

These three are structural, not cosmetic: each one breaks a problem that hurt one of the prior scenarios. Owned land beats Fallbrook's lease drag. Retail node beats SouthPointe's host-dependency. Regional DCFC gap supports 4 DCFC stalls instead of 2-3.

Three structural advantages, each tied to a problem that the other two site scenarios couldn't solve. This is the key insight in the deck.

INCENTIVES · 30C IS GONE

No federal credit.
Pursue state & utility instead.

§30C deadline is real

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (signed July 2025) accelerated §30C termination to property placed in service on or before June 30, 2026 — 47 days from this deck.

~6-9 morealistic permit → build → commission timeline
$0§30C credit value assumed in this capital plan

Substitute funding paths

Counterfactual: at 30% with PW/A compliance on ~$700K eligible equipment, the credit would have been ~$210K. Substitutes:

  • Nebraska Energy Office state grant ($25–50K typical)
  • LES load-management / make-ready rebate (utility-side)
  • SBA 7(a) at favorable terms — owned land is collateral
  • Section 179 + bonus depreciation on equipment

Sources: IRS §30C overview, H.R.1 (OBBBA) text. Owner action: confirm §30C status with CPA before any external deck use.

The SBA path is more meaningful here than at Fallbrook or SouthPointe because owned land is real collateral. Worth pricing 7(a) terms early.

Verify 12-stall layout fits with ADA, snow storage, fire access, and back-in geometry for pickups/SUVs.

Confirm owned-lot status, easements, cross-access agreements, parking covenants.

Pull current AFDC/PlugShare for 10-mi radius to document the no-DCFC claim with a date-stamped record.

Two weekend traffic observations + 30-driver intercept survey at Sam's / Walmart / Menards parking.

6 · Vendor quotes

Price 100 kW vs 150 kW DCFC. If 150 kW raises demand-charge exposure materially, hold at 100 kW or add battery buffer.
