# Customer Discovery — Volt & Go Lincoln EV Charging Station

## Purpose

Track customer interviews, surveys, observations, and validation status for the Lincoln, NE EV charging station.

## Target Customer Hypotheses

### Segment 1: Highway Travelers (DCFC Primary)
- **Customer:** Out-of-state and in-state drivers on I-80, especially those in EVs on longer trips
- **Economic buyer:** The driver; business expense for commercial EV drivers
- **End user:** The driver and passengers
- **Pain point:** Range anxiety on long I-80 trips; limited DCFC options at Lincoln I-80 exits; charging stops that feel unsafe or unwelcoming
- **Current alternative:** Electrify America at specific truck stops; Tesla Superchargers (Tesla owners); driving out of route to find charging
- **Trigger for adoption:** Visible, well-lit, easy-access DCFC location at a convenient I-80 exit; PlugShare/AFDC app listing

### Segment 2: High-End Apartment Residents (L2 Primary)
- **Customer:** EV-owning residents in Class A apartments in Lincoln's south/southwest corridor
- **Economic buyer:** The resident; potential apartment HOA or management co-payment
- **End user:** The EV-owning resident
- **Pain point:** Cannot install Level 2 charger at home (no garage or dedicated outlet); reliant on slow Level 1 (120V) trickle charging or expensive trips to public stations; "charging anxiety" about overnight charge levels
- **Current alternative:** Slow Level 1 charging in apartment lot; ChargePoint/Blink if already in lot; driving to downtown chargers
- **Trigger for adoption:** Close proximity (walkable or <0.5 miles); monthly membership pass; reliable availability; good lighting and security

### Segment 3: Local Commuters and City Residents (L2 Primary)
- **Customer:** Lincoln residents who commute with an EV but lack home Level 2 charging (e.g., townhome, older housing stock)
- **Economic buyer:** The driver
- **End user:** The driver
- **Pain point:** Need a reliable mid-day or evening top-up; want affordable option vs. home charger installation cost
- **Current alternative:** Existing ChargePoint/Blink L2 stations across Lincoln; hotel destination chargers
- **Trigger for adoption:** Competitive per-kWh pricing; monthly membership; proximity to their commute corridor
