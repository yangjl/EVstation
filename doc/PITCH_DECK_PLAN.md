# Pitch Deck Plan — Volt & Go Lincoln EV Charging Station

## Purpose

Plan the HTML-PPT investor pitch deck narrative, slide structure, evidence needs, and review status.

## Audience

- **Primary:** Angel investors, SBA lenders, regional impact investors interested in infrastructure + clean energy
- **Secondary:** City of Lincoln economic development office; Nebraska energy grant program officers

## Deck Narrative Arc

1. The EV revolution is arriving in Nebraska — but the infrastructure isn't keeping up
2. Lincoln's I-80 corridor + high-end apartment belt has a specific, solvable gap
3. Volt & Go Lincoln fills that gap with 10 chargers (8 L2 + 2 DCFC), $1M capital, serving two overlapping customer segments
4. A local, community-invested owner with open-standard hardware outcompetes national chains on convenience and relationships
5. Supported by federal (NEVI) and state grants, a clear revenue model, and growing EV adoption
6. We're asking for [X] to get to site control, permits, and construction start

## Go-to-Market Slide Detail

**Pre-launch (Months 1–6):**
- Identify and secure site (target: I-80 exit near Wilderness Hills / Fallbrook area or similar Class A residential cluster)
- Apply for NEVI grant through Nebraska DOT
- File for IRA §30C tax credit (30% of equipment cost)
- Engage apartment property managers for resident discount program MOUs
- Contractor selection and permitting

**Launch (Month 7–12):**
- Register station on PlugShare, AFDC, Google Maps, Apple Maps, ChargePoint (if using their network)
- Grand opening event; local press
- Resident membership pass direct mail/digital campaign to target apartment complexes
- Lincoln Chamber of Commerce / business community engagement

**Growth (Year 2+):**
- Optimize pricing based on utilization data
- Explore food/retail co-tenancy at site
- Expand NEVI grant application for additional chargers if utilization exceeds projections
