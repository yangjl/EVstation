# Financial Model Notes — Volt & Go Lincoln EV Charging Station

## Purpose

Document the assumptions, inputs, and review status for revenue, cost, margin, growth, and funding scenarios.

> **All figures below are planning estimates based on industry benchmarks. They are NOT validated projections. Human review is required before sharing with investors or lenders.**

## Model Scope

- **Planning horizon:** 5 years (2026–2031)
- **Currency:** USD
- **Revenue streams:** Pay-per-use charging (L2 + DCFC), monthly membership passes (future), potential advertising/retail co-tenancy (future)
- **Cost categories:** Land lease, electricity, hardware depreciation, maintenance, network software, labor/management, insurance, marketing
- **Funding assumptions:** $1M total investment; potential $100K–$200K NEVI or state grant offset
- **Scenario names:** Base Case, Optimistic (30% higher utilization), Conservative (30% lower utilization)

## Sensitivity: Land Lease Reduction

A lower lease cost (e.g., land ownership, city partnership, or $2,500/month lease) materially changes the economics:

| Land Lease | Y3 EBITDA | Y5 EBITDA | Breakeven year |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------------|
| $4,500/month (base) | ($33,520) | ($14,690) | ~Year 7+ |
| $3,000/month | $2,980 | $20,310 | ~Year 3 |
| $2,000/month | $15,480 | $32,810 | ~Year 2–3 |
| Owned land | $48,480 | $65,810 | Year 2 |

> **HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED:** The path to profitability is highly sensitive to land cost. Consider: (a) negotiating ownership stake or below-market lease with apartment developer as co-investment; (b) city/county land partnership; (c) co-location with existing commercial property.

## Funding Sources & Capital Stack

| Source | Amount | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Owner equity | $600,000–$800,000 | Primary capital source |
| SBA 7(a) or 504 loan | $200,000–$400,000 | Equipment and real estate financing; competitive rates |

| Federal tax credit (IRA §30C) | $30,000–$100,000 | 30% of EV charging equipment cost; $100K cap/item |
| **Total potential non-equity funding** | **$355,000–$750,000** | Availability and terms require verification |

> **HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED:** Grant availability, eligibility, and timelines must be verified with Nebraska DOT and a grant consultant before including in a capital plan or investor materials.
