# Business Plan Brief — Volt & Go Lincoln EV Charging Station

**Date drafted:** 2026-05-13
**Status:** Draft — human review required before investor use

---

## The Opportunity in One Paragraph

Lincoln, Nebraska sits on Interstate 80 — the primary transcontinental east-west corridor — while simultaneously growing a high-income, apartment-dense residential population that owns EVs but lacks home charging access. Public DC fast charging near convenient I-80 interchanges adjacent to Class A apartment communities is structurally undersupplied today. A locally-owned, open-standard, 10-charger public station combining DCFC for highway travelers with Level 2 for apartment residents captures two revenue streams from the same physical location with $1M in capital.

---

## Station Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Location | Lincoln, NE — near I-80 interchange + Class A apartments |
| Total chargers | 10 |
| Level 2 chargers | 8 units, 7.2 kW each |
| DC Fast Chargers | 2 units, 50–150 kW each |
| Standards | CCS, CHAdeMO, J1772 (all EV brands) |
| Total investment | ~$1,000,000 |
| Business model | Pay-per-kWh + monthly membership passes |

## Customer Segments

1. **Highway travelers** — DCFC users on I-80; need fast, reliable 20–35 min charge
2. **Apartment residents** — L2 users; no home charger access; monthly membership target
3. **Local commuters** — L2 users; need flexible public charging

---

## Biggest Risks

1. **Land cost** — must validate actual lease/purchase options before proceeding
2. **Utilization ramp** — depends on local EV adoption speed; needs market validation
3. **Grant eligibility** — NEVI status must be confirmed with Nebraska DOT
4. **Competitive entrants** — Electrify America or ChargePoint may enter same corridor
